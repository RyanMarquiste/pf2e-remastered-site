# Foundry VTT export (PF2e system)

This folder holds two Foundry modules. **Marquiste Additions** (`marquiste-additions/`) is the functional module: the scripts and the rules items they use. **CorpseCap's Cauldron** (`corpsecaps-cauldron/`) is the content module: the custom alchemical items. The content module needs the functional module, and Foundry asks you to turn it on when you enable the Cauldron. The functional module has three compendiums, and its scripts are sorted the same way:

- **Common Enhancements** (`scripts/common/`): rules that apply across the whole game. Right now that is the injury poison rules (poisoning a weapon, and using the poison up when a Strike hits or critically fails), the Fleeting trait (which removes what carries it at the end of the turn), the Mutable traits, the Formula trait with the formula books on the Crafting tab, and the Compendium Browser setup. The pack holds the mutagens copied from PF2e (Drakeheart, Cognitive and Juggernaut) and a formula for each. None of this is specific to Alchemists, so the scripts are in `scripts/common/`; the three formula items that the books use are in the CorpseCap's Cauldron module, because they describe its items.
- **Alchemist Enhancements** (`scripts/alchemist/`): features only for Alchemists: the Volatile Vial, the Field Vials, the Toxic Coatings, the Toxicologist's Mutable Acid, the Quick Vial, Create Consumable and Quick Alchemy (the Marquiste action) actions, and a formula item for each of PF2e's mutagen families that start at level 1 (16 of them, listed in `mutagen-formulas.js`; each covers the Lesser, Moderate, Greater and Major tiers), and a formula item for each of PF2e's other tiered alchemical items listed in `alchemical-formulas.js` (28 families: **Acid Flask** [4], **Alchemist's Fire** [4], **Antidote** [4], **Antiplague** [4], **Blasting Stone** [4], **Blight Bomb** [4], **Blood Booster** [3], **Bottled Lightning** [4], **Bottled Sunlight** [4], **Boulder Seed** [2], **Comprehension Elixir** [2], **Crystal Shards** [3], **Darkvision Elixir** [3], **Dread Ampoule** [4], **Elixir of Life** [6], **Frost Vial** [4], **Ghost Charge** [4], **Glue Bomb** [4], **Infiltrator's Elixir** [1, so a formula item with one tier], **Lodestone Bomb** [2], **Necrotic Bomb** [4], **Numbing Tonic** [6], **Shatterstone** [2], **Skunk Bomb** [4], **Soothing Tonic** [4], **Spiderfoot Brew** [3], **Sticky Algae Bomb** [4], **Trueshape Bomb** [2]; each formula is priced at its lowest tier, the bombs are the common alchemical bombs, and a family is one more entry in that file). Built by `build.js` from `vials.js`, `mutagen-formulas.js` and `alchemical-formulas.js`. The pack's id is still `marquiste-alchemist-class`, so links from existing characters keep working.
- **Bard Enhancements** (`scripts/bard/`): features only for Bards. Right now that is the three **Versatile Performance** actions (Make an Impression, Demoralize and Impersonate, rolled with Performance), built by `build.js` from `bard.js`. See Versatile Performance below.
- **Wandering Chef Enhancements** (`scripts/wandering-chef/`): the **Wandering Chef Dedication** with its **food packets**, **Quick Cooking** (Create Food or **Flash Cook**), the **Cooking Toolkit**, and the **Food Projectile** that Flash Cook creates (4 tiers at levels 1, 4, 12 and 18, scaled like the Volatile Vial but without splash), built by `build.js` from `wandering-chef.js`. See Wandering Chef below.

The **CorpseCap's Cauldron** module has two compendiums. The first has this site's own alchemical items, the Mind Doubling and Ichthys mutagens and Insulating Paste. (The mutagens copied from PF2e are in Marquiste Additions, in its Common Enhancements pack.) The second, **Cauldron Formulas**, has one formula item for each of those families, carrying the Formula trait, so a formula can be found by trait in the Compendium Browser and put in a formula book. It has no scripts of its own: the Insulating Paste counter is in Marquiste Additions (`scripts/cauldron/`), because it is behaviour, not content.

Both modules are built by `build.js`. The Cauldron pack has the Mind Doubling and Ichthys mutagens and Insulating Paste, each with 4 consumables (Lesser, Moderate, Greater, Major, priced and levelled like the other mutagens) and 4 matching `Effect:` items that apply the numbers through PF2e rule elements:

- **Mind Doubling Mutagen**: per tier, a main `Effect: Mind Doubling Mutagen` and a `... Mutant Innervation` effect, plus one shared `Effect: Mind Doubling Mutagen Drawback` that doesn't change with tier. The main effect carries the benefits (item bonuses) and grants the **Co-ordinated Thinking** free action, the shared Drawback (the penalties and weakness 5 to mental) and, only for characters with the Mutant Innervation feat, the Mutant Innervation effect (the saving throw re-roll)
- **Drakeheart Mutagen** (in Marquiste Additions' Common Enhancements pack): PF2e's Drakeheart Mutagen (same art, text, levels, prices and numbers) with the benefit and the drawback as separate effects, like the Mind Doubling Mutagen. Per tier, `Effect: Drakeheart Mutagen` carries the item bonus to AC, the Dexterity cap and the Perception bonus, and grants PF2e's own **Final Surge** action and the shared `Effect: Drakeheart Mutagen Drawback` (the -1 to Will and Reflex saves and to Recall Knowledge). It has a **Formula: Drakeheart Mutagen** item in the Cauldron Formulas pack that covers all four tiers
- **Cognitive Mutagen** (in Marquiste Additions' Common Enhancements pack): PF2e's Cognitive Mutagen (Player Core 2: same art, text, levels 1/3/11/17, prices, durations and numbers) with the benefit and the drawback as separate effects, like the Drakeheart Mutagen. (Built by `foundry/split-mutagen.js` from a small spec file for each mutagen, so another mutagen is one more spec file.) Per tier there is the consumable and a benefit effect (the +1/+2/+3/+4 item bonus to Arcana, Crafting, Lore, Occultism and Society and to Recall Knowledge, and critical failures on Recall Knowledge become failures), which grants one shared **Effect: Cognitive Mutagen Drawback** (the -2 penalty to weapon and unarmed attack rolls, Athletics and Acrobatics, 2 less Bulk before encumbered and 4 less maximum Bulk) that the Mutagenist Field Vial can suppress. One fix to PF2e's data: its Major effect lowers the Bulk limit before encumbered twice and never lowers the maximum Bulk, so every tier here lowers the maximum Bulk by 4 as the text says. The Greater and Major trained-skill line is text only, as in PF2e. The names match PF2e's; the Marquiste trait and Source line tell them apart, and the slugs keep a "marquiste" (for example `cognitive-mutagen-marquiste-lesser`). **Formula: Cognitive Mutagen** in the same pack covers the four tiers. The Alchemist pack still has its own formula for PF2e's Cognitive Mutagen.
- **Juggernaut Mutagen** (in Marquiste Additions' Common Enhancements pack): PF2e's Juggernaut Mutagen (Player Core 2: same art, text, levels 1/3/11/17, prices and durations) with the benefit and the drawback as separate effects, built the same way as the Cognitive Mutagen. Per tier there is the consumable and a benefit effect (a +1/+2/+3/+4 item bonus to Fortitude saves and 5/10/30/45 temporary Hit Points, and on Greater and Major success on a Fortitude save becomes a critical success, with critical failures becoming failures on Major), which grants one shared **Effect: Juggernaut Mutagen Drawback** (a -2 penalty to Will saves, Perception checks and initiative rolls) that the Mutagenist Field Vial can suppress. The item's line about regaining the temporary Hit Points after a minute at full health is text only, as in PF2e. One fix to PF2e's text: the Moderate item said "10 minute", and here it says 10 minutes. **Formula: Juggernaut Mutagen** covers the four tiers, and the Alchemist pack still has its own formula for PF2e's.
- **Serene Mutagen** (in Marquiste Additions' Common Enhancements pack): PF2e's Serene Mutagen (Player Core 2: same art, text, levels 1/3/11/17, prices, durations and numbers) with the benefit and the drawback as separate effects, built the same way as the Cognitive Mutagen. Per tier there is the consumable and a benefit effect (a +1/+2/+3/+4 item bonus to Will saves and Perception, Medicine, Nature, Religion and Survival checks, which is 1 higher on Will saves against mental effects for the Lesser, Moderate and Greater; on the Greater a success on a Will save against a mental effect becomes a critical success, and on the Major critical failures on those saves also become failures), which grants one shared **Effect: Serene Mutagen Drawback** (a -1 penalty to attack rolls, and to spell save DCs through PF2e's own toggle on the effect, and a -1 penalty per damage die to weapon and unarmed damage) that the Mutagenist Field Vial can suppress. The penalty to spell damage is in the text only, as in PF2e's own effect, and so is the Mutant Innervation benefit (detection, revelation and scrying of 9th rank or lower detect nothing from you). The effects' text matches the items' (PF2e's effects leave out "item" and spell damage). **Formula: Serene Mutagen** covers the four tiers, and the Alchemist pack still has its own formula for PF2e's.
- **Quicksilver Mutagen** (in Marquiste Additions' Common Enhancements pack): PF2e's Quicksilver Mutagen (Player Core 2: same art, text, levels 1/3/11/17, prices, durations and numbers) with the benefit and the drawback as separate effects, built the same way as the Cognitive Mutagen. Per tier there is the consumable and a benefit effect (a +1/+2/+3/+4 item bonus to Acrobatics, Stealth and Thievery checks, Reflex saves and Dexterity-based attack rolls, and a +5/+10/+15/+20 foot status bonus to Speed), which grants one shared **Effect: Quicksilver Mutagen Drawback** (a -2 penalty to Fortitude saves, and damage equal to twice the character's level that can't be recovered while the mutagen lasts, PF2e's own `LoseHitPoints` rule) that the Mutagenist Field Vial can suppress. The damage is taken once, when the drawback effect is applied, so the Mutagenist Field Vial can't skip it: suppressing the drawback afterwards ends the Fortitude penalty and lets the Hit Points be recovered, but does not give the damage back. The Mutant Physique benefit (Step up to 10 feet, and squeeze through spaces as one size smaller) is text only, as in PF2e. **Formula: Quicksilver Mutagen** covers the four tiers, and the Alchemist pack still has its own formula for PF2e's.
- **Bestial Mutagen** (in Marquiste Additions' Common Enhancements pack): PF2e's Bestial Mutagen (Player Core 2: same art, text, levels 1/3/11/17, prices, durations and numbers) with the benefit and the drawback as separate effects, built the same way as the Cognitive Mutagen. Per tier there is the consumable and a benefit effect (a +1/+2/+3/+4 item bonus to Athletics checks and unarmed attack rolls, and PF2e's own Strike rules for the claw, 1d4/2d6/3d8/4d8 slashing and agile, and the jaws, 1d6/2d8/3d10/4d10 piercing, which carry PF2e's `bestial-mutagen-strike` tag; the Major also has PF2e's weapon specialization rules for them), which grants one shared **Effect: Bestial Mutagen Drawback** (a -2 penalty to Reflex saves, Acrobatics and Stealth) that the Mutagenist Field Vial can suppress. One fix to PF2e's data: its Moderate claw has no slug, as the others have, so here it has one. The Mutant Physique feat's damage die step and deadly d10 come from PF2e's own feat, which finds the tag on the claw and jaws; the copy gives the Intimidation bonus itself. **Formula: Bestial Mutagen** covers the four tiers, and the Alchemist pack still has its own formula for PF2e's.
- **Theatrical Mutagen** (in Marquiste Additions' Common Enhancements pack): PF2e's Theatrical Mutagen (Player Core 2: same art, text, levels 1/3/11/17 and numbers) with the benefit and the drawback as separate effects, built the same way as the Cognitive Mutagen. It does not follow the shared tiers, and the copy keeps PF2e's own prices and durations: the Lesser costs 3 gp (the others 12, 300 and 3,000 gp), and the effects last 1 minute, 1 minute, 10 minutes and 1 hour, with the duration in the item's flavor line and no Duration line. Per tier there is the consumable and a benefit effect (a +1/+2/+3/+4 item bonus to Acrobatics, Crafting and Performance checks, a proficiency bonus equal to the character's level to those skills, which only matters to someone untrained in them, and a +5/+5/+10/+10 foot status bonus to Speed), which grants one shared **Effect: Theatrical Mutagen Drawback** (a -1 penalty to Perception checks and Will saves) that the Mutagenist Field Vial can suppress. The drawback's Off-Guard after a round without an Interact, Perform, Step or Stride is text only, as in PF2e. **Formula: Theatrical Mutagen** covers the four tiers, and the Alchemist pack still has its own formula for PF2e's.
- **Insulating Paste**: a choice of acid, cold, electricity or fire when the effect is applied (the same way PF2e's Energy Mutagen does it), resistance 5/10/15/20 to that type, and a badge counter of 5 instances of protection
- **Volatile Vial** (Lesser, Moderate, Greater, Major; levels 1/4/12/18): the throwable bomb, built from PF2e's own Versatile Vial data so the attack bonus and dice scale the same way. It is created by Quick Vial and is separate from the versatile vial resource
- **Field Vials**: **Bomber** (4 tiers; acid by default, and the Strike can switch to cold, electricity or fire through the Versatile Cold, Electricity and Fire traits, which also changes the splash damage), **Toxicologist** (4 poison bombs, each with a **Toxic Coating** and an **Advanced Toxic Coating** effect), **Chirurgeon** (4 healing tiers) and **Mutagenist** (a single item and effect, with the Advanced Vial physical resistance)
- **Quick Vial**: an action for Alchemists. Using it opens a picker (styled like PF2e's formula picker, with each vial's icon, traits and a check button) showing the best tier the character can make of each Volatile Vial and research-field Field Vial, with a **Show all tiers** button at the bottom that lists the lower tiers too. A Toxicologist also gets **Toxic Coating** (and **Advanced Toxic Coating** from 11th level) for each tier: picking one asks which weapon to coat and applies that effect to the character instead of creating a vial and puts it in a free hand (nothing is created if no hand is free). In combat the vial is removed when the creator's turn ends
- **Create Consumable**: an action for Alchemists. Using it expends one versatile vial, asks which alchemical consumable formula (your level or lower, and not one with the Processed trait) to create, and adds the infused item to the character; in combat it is removed when the creator's next turn starts
- **Formula books** (Common Enhancements; `scripts/common/formula-trait.js`, `formula-books.js`, `crafting-tab.js`): PF2e's own **Formula Book (Blank)** item holds formulas, and its groups show above Known Formulas on the Crafting tab, each with the highest level formulas at the top. Each group has two buttons that open the Compendium Browser's Equipment tab: **Items** (everything) and **Formulas** (with the Formula trait already chosen). Drag what you find onto the book. A book holds two kinds of entry: a **formula item** (an item with the **Formula** trait, one for each tiered family, in the Cauldron Formulas pack), which teaches every tier at or below the character's level, so the next tier arrives on levelling up; and any **plain item**, which teaches just that item. Drop either kind on a book's group, or use the book icon on a formula row to move it between books (or to no book). A formula's book is its only group, a formula no book gives stays in Known Formulas, and formulas a player added by hand are never taken away when a book changes. Deleting a book (or removing a formula from it with the trash icon) makes the character forget what it gave. The alchemist field vials are not formulas: only the Quick Vial action makes them.
- **Mutable traits** (Common Enhancements; Mutable Acid, Mutable Poison, Mutable Fire and so on, one per damage type): like Versatile, but the target decides. Damage of the item's own type can change to the named type against a creature for which it is better. The Toxicologist Field Vials, the Toxic Coatings and any infused poison item a Toxicologist creates have **Mutable Acid**
- **Quick Alchemy**: an action (adding it to a character also adds the Quick Vial and Create Consumable actions, and they are removed with it) with the revised Quick Alchemy text (Quick Vial doesn't expend a versatile vial, and items last at most 10 minutes). With the Quick Alchemy setting on, using it asks you to choose **Quick Vial** or **Create Consumable** (shown but unavailable while you have no versatile vial left); with the setting off it does nothing
- **Ichthys Mutagen**: swim Speed equal to land Speed, an item bonus to unarmed attacks, a slashing "Razor Claw" unarmed Strike, a toggleable "Underwater" option for the Perception and Wisdom-skill bonus, the Athletics penalty, and weakness 5 to electricity

The files were written against the PF2e system's own mutagen data (Cognitive Mutagen) but have **not been loaded into a running Foundry yet**, so expect to check them once (see "Check it" below).

## Install

Foundry v11+ only reads compendiums stored as LevelDB folders, so the JSON has to be compiled once.

1. **Compile the packs.** From the repo root, with Node installed, run all five commands (the Cauldron pack is in its own module folder):

   ```bash
   npx @foundryvtt/foundryvtt-cli package pack -n marquiste-common-enhancements --type Module --in foundry/marquiste-additions/packs/_source/marquiste-common-enhancements --out foundry/marquiste-additions/packs
   ```

   ```bash
   npx @foundryvtt/foundryvtt-cli package pack -n corpsecaps-cauldron --type Module --in foundry/corpsecaps-cauldron/packs/_source/corpsecaps-cauldron --out foundry/corpsecaps-cauldron/packs
   npx @foundryvtt/foundryvtt-cli package pack -n corpsecaps-formulas --type Module --in foundry/corpsecaps-cauldron/packs/_source/corpsecaps-formulas --out foundry/corpsecaps-cauldron/packs
   ```

   ```bash
   npx @foundryvtt/foundryvtt-cli package pack -n marquiste-alchemist-class --type Module --in foundry/marquiste-additions/packs/_source/marquiste-alchemist-class --out foundry/marquiste-additions/packs
   ```

   ```bash
   npx @foundryvtt/foundryvtt-cli package pack -n marquiste-bard-class --type Module --in foundry/marquiste-additions/packs/_source/marquiste-bard-class --out foundry/marquiste-additions/packs
   ```

   ```bash
   npx @foundryvtt/foundryvtt-cli package pack -n marquiste-wandering-chef --type Module --in foundry/marquiste-additions/packs/_source/marquiste-wandering-chef --out foundry/marquiste-additions/packs
   ```

   This creates `foundry/marquiste-additions/packs/marquiste-common-enhancements/`, `.../marquiste-alchemist-class/`, `.../marquiste-bard-class/` and `.../marquiste-wandering-chef/`, and `foundry/corpsecaps-cauldron/packs/corpsecaps-cauldron/` and `.../corpsecaps-formulas/`. 

2. **Copy both module folders**, `foundry/marquiste-additions` and `foundry/corpsecaps-cauldron`, into your Foundry data folder, so you end up with `Data/modules/marquiste-additions/module.json` and `Data/modules/corpsecaps-cauldron/module.json`. (Foundry's data folder is shown under Configuration in the setup screen. On Windows it's usually `%localappdata%\FoundryVTT\Data`.)

3. In your world, go to **Settings → Manage Modules**, enable **Marquiste Additions** and **CorpseCap's Cauldron**, and save. (Enabling the Cauldron on its own makes Foundry offer to turn on Marquiste Additions too.)

4. Open the **Compendium Packs** tab. The **Common Enhancements**, **Alchemist Enhancements**, **Bard Enhancements** and **Wandering Chef Enhancements** packs appear under Marquiste Additions, and the **CorpseCap's Cauldron** and **Cauldron Formulas** packs under CorpseCap's Cauldron. Drag a mutagen onto a character sheet.

Both `module.json` files ask for Foundry v13 as the minimum and are verified on v14 (build 370, with PF2e 8.6.1). If yours is a different version, change `compatibility` in `build.js` and rebuild.

## Check it

When you first use the item:

1. Drag **Mind Doubling Mutagen (Lesser)** onto a character and use it. The linked **Effect** should apply and show on the character.
2. While the effect is active, the sheet should list **Co-ordinated Thinking**, and it should go away when the effect ends.
3. Check the numbers on the sheet: +1 to Perception and Intelligence-based skills, +1 to Will saves against mental effects, −1 to Acrobatics, Stealth and Thievery, −2 to Reflex, and weakness 5 to mental damage.
4. If the character has the Mutant Innervation feat, Will saves against mental effects should roll twice and keep the higher result.

## Keeping the Alchemist items out of the equipment list

PF2e can't hide single items; its Compendium Browser lists whole packs per tab. So the Alchemist-only items are in their own pack, and `scripts/common/compendium-browser.js` switches that pack off for the Compendium Browser's **Equipment** tab the first time the module runs (it stays on for the Actions tab). To change it, open the Compendium Browser's settings and tick the pack for the Equipment tab. The module only sets this once, so your choice sticks. The pack is still in the Compendium sidebar. The mutagen formula items are in that pack too, so with the pack switched off they do not appear when you filter the Equipment tab by the Formula trait; open the pack from the Compendium sidebar and drag them onto a formula book from there, or tick the pack for the Equipment tab (which also lists the vials there).

## Module settings

Open **Settings → Configure Settings → Marquiste Additions**. The options are world settings, and a change takes effect straight away without a reload. Turning one off stops the module acting; it never deletes what the feature made earlier (poison effects, formula books, known formulas). The Formula trait is not an option: it only adds a name to PF2e's trait lists. The Fleeting and Mutable traits can be switched off under Troubleshooting (below).

The settings window groups them under headings:

| Heading | Setting | Default | When it is off |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Poisons** | **Application** | on | Injury poisons work as in PF2e. When on, using one asks which weapon to poison, adds a Poisoned Weapon effect, asks before replacing a poison already on that weapon, and posts the poison's description and save when the weapon hits. The Alchemist's Toxic Coatings are separate. |
| **Poisons** | **Removal** | on | The effect stays on the weapon until you delete it. When on, the first successful Strike or a critical failure uses it up (on ammunition, firing the weapon does). Covers injury poisons and Toxic Coatings. |
| **Crafting** | **Formula Books** | on | The Crafting tab is PF2e's own and nothing is synced. Formulas already learned stay known. Turning it back on brings every character with a book up to date. |
| **Alchemist** | **Quick Alchemy** | off | See below. |
| **Alchemist → Toxicologist** | **Mutable Acid** | on | Items are left as PF2e creates them. |
| **Alchemist → Toxicologist** | **Infused Poison Expiry** | on | The poison effect stays on the weapon after resting. |
| **Splash** | **Splash targets** | on | Splash works as in PF2e. See Splash targets below. |
| **Splash** | **Apply splash automatically** | off | Each creature's row has a Splash button. When on, the active GM's screen applies the splash as soon as the damage is rolled. |
| **Wandering Chef** | **Wandering Chef enhancements** | on | Quick Cooking, Create Food, Create Processed Food and Flash Cook do nothing. See Wandering Chef below. |
| **Game Master** | **Call for Recall Knowledge** | on | The Call for Recall Knowledge macro and its Recall Knowledge button do nothing. See Call for Recall Knowledge below. |
| **Cauldron** | **Insulating Paste** | on | No chat message with the "use up one instance" button. |
| **Troubleshooting** | **Troubleshooting mode** | off | The switches below it are hidden and ignored. See Troubleshooting below. |

Foundry's settings window has no headings of its own, so `scripts/common/settings.js` adds them (and puts the settings in this order) when the window opens. If a Foundry update changes the window's markup, the settings still work and just appear in a plain list.

### Troubleshooting

When the module clashes with another module, tick **Troubleshooting mode** under **Troubleshooting**. A switch appears for each feature, all on. Switch features off one at a time until the problem goes away, and you have found which part clashes.

- **Fleeting trait** and **Mutable traits**: the two traits' behaviour. With either off, the trait is only a label.
- **Crafting tab layout**: the formula books drawn on the Crafting tab (the tab becomes PF2e's own).
- **Formula sync**: the module changing a character's formula list.
- **Alchemist actions**: Quick Vial, Create Consumable, the Quick Alchemy (the Marquiste action) chooser, and removing what they create at the end of the turn.
- **Bomb vial upkeep**: marking a thrown vial as used and removing it afterwards.
- **Compendium Browser pack**: the one-time change that hides the Alchemist pack from the Equipment tab.
- **Log activity**: writes what the module is doing to the browser console (F12).

These switches only count while Troubleshooting mode is on. Turn it off and every one is ignored, so everything runs normally, whatever the switches were left at. While it is on and anything is off, the GM is told when the world loads.

### Splash targets

A weapon with the **Splash** trait (the alchemical bombs; `scripts/common/bomb-splash.js`, with the distance arithmetic in `splash-geometry.js`) has its splash targets worked out for you instead of leaving it to the table. PF2e's damage on a hit is not changed; the module finds who else is splashed:

1. **The reach.** 5 feet from the target, and 5 feet more for each of **Expanded Splash** (switched on in the character's rolls toggles), the **Splash 10 ft** trait, and the bomb's critical specialization on a critical hit, up to 15 feet. It is measured from the target to the nearest square of each creature, with PF2e's rule for diagonals (5 feet, then 10, then 5). Everyone counts: allies, the thrower and hidden creatures. A large target is measured from its whole space.
2. **Everyone else** within reach is listed in the damage roll's own chat message, under PF2e's buttons, but only when the attack was a hit or a critical hit (a miss splashes no one else). Each creature gets a row with its picture and the amount it takes, and a single **Splash** button; the button greys out once used. Each takes the splash once. Splash is never halved or doubled, so there is no Half, Double or Block. A player can only press them for creatures they can change; otherwise the GM presses them. With **Apply splash automatically** on, the active GM's screen applies it as the damage appears and the rows say so. The separate splash roll PF2e attaches to the damage card is dropped while this is on, so the splash cannot be applied twice. The card heading says how far it reached and what widened it.
3. **A miss.** Whether it was a miss is read from the attack roll, because PF2e's Damage button on the attack card always rolls damage as a hit: after a miss the damage roll becomes just the splash (labelled Miss), taken once by the target. A critical failure deals no splash.

It works on square-grid scenes where the target is on the scene being viewed. On a hex or gridless scene the roll is left as PF2e made it. The **Splash targets** setting turns all of this off.

### Call for Recall Knowledge

The **Marquiste Macros** compendium (GM only) has a **Call for Recall Knowledge** macro; drag it to the hotbar. ### Wandering Chef

**Wandering Chef Dedication** replaces the dedication from the Tian Xia Character Guide, so use it instead of that one (it is a separate item and does not touch PF2e's). Add the dedication to an existing Wandering Chef in place of the official one; remove the official dedication's versatile vial and Quick Alchemy with it.

### Expiry outside combat

What Create Consumable, Create Food and Quick Vial make, and the Flash Cook Food Projectile, last until the start of the creator's next turn (the projectile and vials, the end of their turn). Anything else with the Fleeting trait that lands on a character or scene is stamped the same way by `scripts/common/fleeting-trait.js`, which also removes it when the combat ends or the clock moves on. `lifetimeStamp` and `expireWithClock` in `scripts/common/shared.js` handle what a turn can't: the item is stamped when it is made (the combat, combatant and round in a combat; outside combat, the world time a round later, `CONFIG.time.roundTime`, 6 seconds). The active GM's client then removes the item when a combat ends (everything made in that combat) and when the world clock moves forward to or past a stamped time outside combat (`updateWorldTime`, which Foundry's +10 minutes and +1 hour clock buttons trigger; going backwards does nothing). Items made outside combat by an earlier version have no time, so they are removed the next time the clock moves forward. Create Processed Food is stamped for as long as the food stays potent: its activation time when that is a length of time (10 minutes, 1 hour; read from the item's description with `activationSeconds`), or a round when it takes actions, and the chat message says which. In or out of combat, the world clock decides. Fleeting is part of its own setting (Troubleshooting, Fleeting trait), and the others of Alchemist actions and Wandering Chef enhancements.

### Makeshift and Broken

`scripts/common/broken-trait.js` adds the **Broken** trait and `scripts/common/makeshift.js` adds the **Makeshift** trait, each to PF2e's weapon, armor, shield and equipment trait lists (Broken also to the consumable list), with a hover description, and acts on it. Each is one setting (**Broken items** and **Makeshift items**; off: only a label).

**Broken** is a trait on the item, not a state worked out from Hit Points as PF2e's own broken is, so it works for an item that has no Hit Points. An item with it can no longer be used for its intended purpose:
- **Weapon:** it can't Strike. The module wraps PF2e's `Check.roll` (through libWrapper when that is installed, so it sits with the other modules that wrap it, and directly otherwise) and a Strike with a broken weapon is cancelled with a warning before it is rolled. The weapon stays in the hand, so the character must stow it and draw another.
- **Shield:** the item reports `isBroken` while it has the trait (the module patches the getter on PF2e's physical item class, so the trait counts as well as Hit Points), so PF2e's own broken-shield rules apply: no AC bonus, and it can't be raised.
- **Tools and other items that give a bonus:** a broken item's rule elements are not applied (the module wraps the item's `prepareRuleElements`), so a bonus it gives through rules is gone while it is broken.
- **Wands and scrolls:** a consumable with a spell in it can't be cast (`castEmbeddedSpell` is blocked with a warning).
- **Armor:** only its rule elements are switched off. PF2e does not apply the broken-armor AC penalty itself, and neither does this module. Staves and other equipment that cast through their own spellcasting are not blocked either.

**Mending it:**
- An item **with Hit Points** loses the trait in the same update that raises its Hit Points above its Broken Threshold (half its maximum): Repair's Restore button, healing, or an edit of the Hit Points all count. Raising them to the threshold or below, or lowering them, does not.
- An item **with no Hit Points**, or with Hit Points already above its threshold (Makeshift breaks an item without lowering them, so there is nothing to raise), loses it when a Repair check on it succeeds. PF2e's Repair action posts a Restore card for a success or critical success, carrying the item's uuid; the active GM's client sees that card and takes the trait off, so a GM has to be connected. The DC is PF2e's own for Repair, by the item's level.
- `breakItem` and `mendItem` in `broken-trait.js` add and remove the trait for other scripts (Makeshift uses them).

**Makeshift** applies Broken instead of lowering Hit Points:
- **Weapon:** a critical failure on a Strike with a makeshift weapon gives it the Broken trait. Its Hit Points, if it has any, are not touched.
- **Shield / armor:** when an attack roll against a character is a critical hit, the character's raised shield breaks if it is makeshift. If no shield is raised, their worn armor breaks if it is makeshift. A raised shield that is not makeshift takes the hit and the armor is left alone.
- **Rerolls:** PF2e rerolls (hero points and other fortune effects) update the same chat message. The attack's chat message records what was broken (a `makeshift` flag with the item's uuid), and if the message's outcome stops being a critical failure or critical hit, the trait is taken off again.
- **Who does the work:** only the active GM's client edits items, because a player can't edit another player's item, so a GM has to be connected.
- **The kitchen weapons** have no Hardness or Hit Points (earlier versions gave them PF2e's thin wood and iron values), so a broken one is mended by a successful Repair check. A copy already in a character's inventory keeps its old Hit Points and Hardness until it is replaced with one from the compendium. It still works: the Broken trait does the breaking, and a successful Repair check mends it since its Hit Points stay above the threshold.

### Fleeting

An item or effect with the **Fleeting** trait that lands on a character is removed when the turn it was created in ends, and so is a creature with the trait that is put on the scene. Outside combat there are no turns, so it is removed when the world clock moves forward a round or more after it was made (see Expiry outside combat), and everything made in a combat also goes when the combat ends. Only the moment of creation starts the clock, so items that already exist are never removed. The vials made by Quick Vial, the items made by Create Consumable and Create Food and the Food Projectile end the way those actions describe and are left alone by this.

### Quick Alchemy

Tick **Quick Alchemy** under **Alchemist** (it was called "Use the Volatile Vial house rules", and keeps its saved value). While it's on, using the **Quick Alchemy** action asks you to choose **Quick Vial** or **Create Consumable** (Create Consumable is shown but unavailable while the character has no versatile vial left). The setting does not touch PF2e's own Quick Alchemy.

An Alchemist's **versatile vials** are still PF2e's resource (the Versatile Vials class feature's `SpecialResource`, with `getResource("versatile-vials")`), but it is tracked by a **Versatile Vial that is not a weapon**. PF2e's resource is the quantity of its Versatile Vial item, which is a bomb; now that the **Volatile Vial** (the throwable bomb, which only exists when **Quick Vial** creates one, placed in a free hand) is the bomb, the module keeps the count on a copy that is equipment and only a count: the **Versatile Vial** in the Alchemist Enhancements pack (slug `versatile-vial`, id `gIGK3zQMwyuj9mlC`, no damage or bomb statistics, no rules). `scripts/alchemist/versatile-vial-resource.js` does the tying:
- It extends PF2e's `SpecialResource` rule element through `game.pf2e.RuleElements.custom`, so a rule whose item is PF2e's Versatile Vial (`Compendium.pf2e.equipment-srd.Item.ljT5pe8D7rudJqus`; the class feature and Alchemical Sciences Methodology both have one) names ours instead. PF2e then does what it always did with the item: it creates it when the feature is added, sets its quantity (and level, 1/4/12/18) at daily preparations and when a vial is spent, and reads the resource from its quantity. Quick Alchemy and Create Consumable keep reading `getResource`, so they are unchanged.
- A character that already has PF2e's Versatile Vial weapon gets ours in its place when the world loads, with the same quantity, level and container, and the weapon is deleted (the active GM's client does it, so a GM has to be connected). An actor made from the feature afterwards gets ours straight away.
- It is the setting **Versatile vials as a resource** (under Alchemist, on by default). Reload after changing it; off puts PF2e's own rule back, though a character already swapped keeps our item until it is deleted. The id in the script (`VERSATILE_VIAL_ID`) is the one `build.js` makes from the seed `item:Versatile Vial (resource)`, and the stub test checks they match.
- Nothing else renames or replaces the vial. If a character still has a Versatile Vial that an earlier version of this module renamed to Volatile Vial, it is put back when the world loads.
- **What this means in play:** the character's Strikes no longer list a Versatile Vial to throw (the Volatile Vial from Quick Vial is the bomb), and the Bomber field's rules that alter the Versatile Vial weapon no longer have anything to alter, since the Volatile Vial and the field vials carry their own.

## Notes on how the rules are built

- **Toxic Coating:** each coating effect asks which weapon is coated (any owned weapon dealing piercing or slashing damage; the Quick Vial picker also requires that it is held or worn, which PF2e's own prompt can't test), adds its poison damage to that weapon's damage only, tags the weapon "poisoned" and "toxic-coating", and adds a note to the weapon's description. The effect follows the Injury trait: it is used up by the first successful Strike and by a critical failure, a miss leaves it on, and it otherwise ends at the end of the turn. `scripts/common/injury-poisons.js` removes it when the coated weapon's damage is rolled (which only happens on a hit), or when its attack roll is a critical failure (this needs PF2e to know the degree of success, so it only works when the Strike has a target to compare against). Ammunition itself can't be chosen, because PF2e gives a damage roll no way to tell which loaded ammunition was fired. Instead you coat the weapon the ammunition is loaded in (it must still deal piercing or slashing damage). On such a weapon the poison is really on the ammunition, so firing it uses the coating up: a miss spends it at once, and a hit spends it after that shot's damage is rolled.
- **Injury poisons:** `scripts/common/injury-poisons.js` handles every injury poison from PF2e's equipment (any consumable in the poison category with the Injury trait). When a character uses one, the module asks which held or worn piercing or slashing weapon to poison (the same picker as the Toxic Coatings), adds a **Poisoned Weapon** effect to the character, tags the weapon "poisoned" and "injury-poison", and adds a note to its description. The effect has no time limit, except that a poison an alchemist made (one with the Infused trait) keeps that trait on the effect and ends at daily preparations (see **Infused poisons** below). It is used up by the first successful Strike (its damage roll) or a critical failure, a miss leaves it on, and on a weapon that fires ammunition it is used up when the weapon is fired. A hit posts the poison's description and saving throw in the chat for the GM to resolve; Foundry does not apply poison afflictions itself. A weapon holds one poison at a time: if the chosen weapon already has an injury poison or a Toxic Coating, a warning asks whether to overwrite it (the same check applies when a Toxicologist applies a Toxic Coating through Quick Vial). Declining keeps the old poison and, for an injury poison, wastes the new one, since PF2e has already used it up. Poisons that are put on a weapon some other way, such as dragging a coating effect onto a character, are not checked. PF2e has already used the poison up by the time the module sees it, so if there is no eligible weapon the poison is wasted. It also spends the Alchemist's Toxic Coatings, or any other effect with the Injury and Poison traits that has chosen a weapon.
- **Mutable traits and the Toxicologist field benefit:** `scripts/common/mutable-trait.js` registers a `Mutable <damage type>` trait for every damage type. When damage is rolled from a weapon with one, the script adds a redirect from the weapon's own damage type to the mutable type. When it is rolled from a weapon with a Mutable effect on it (the Toxic Coatings), the redirect goes from the damage types that effect adds (poison). PF2e's own redirect logic then works for each target: a target immune to the original type takes the mutable type, and a target that resists the original type more than the mutable type takes the mutable type. Weaknesses are not compared. `scripts/alchemist/toxicologist.js` is the Toxicologist feature: any infused item with the poison trait that a Toxicologist creates (Quick Vial, Create Consumable, PF2e's own Quick Alchemy) gets Mutable Acid as it is created. Acid items do not get Mutable Poison. The vials and coatings in the pack have the trait already. PF2e's built-in **Toxicologist field benefit** toggle forces everything to acid, so leave it off. It works on damage rolled through PF2e's own damage rolls. Effects made by using a consumable (rather than by Quick Vial) do not inherit the trait yet, and the traits are visible, not hidden.

- **Formula books:** a book stores its entries in the flag `flags.marquiste-additions.entries` (item uuids, in order). The character's real formula list, `system.crafting.formulas`, is kept in step by `formula-books.js` whenever a book is created, changed or deleted, or the character's level changes. The formulas the script added are remembered in the actor flag `flags.marquiste-additions.bookFormulas`, and only those are ever removed. If an entry cannot be read (for example an item from a module that is turned off) nothing is removed. PF2e adds anything dropped on the Crafting tab as a formula for that very item, so a formula item that turns up in the list is taken out and put in a book. The groups on the sheet move PF2e's own rows, so the Craft button and quantity box keep working, but they depend on PF2e's Crafting tab markup and need revisiting if PF2e redraws the character sheet.
- **Infused poisons:** PF2e's Infused trait says non-permanent effects from infused items end at the next daily preparations, but PF2e only deletes the infused items, not the poison on a weapon. `scripts/alchemist/daily-preparations.js` removes any Poisoned Weapon effect (or other effect with the Infused, Injury and Poison traits) when the character rests for the night, or when daily crafting is reset with the Reset Daily Crafting button (if daily crafting was marked complete). A poison that was not infused, such as one bought or found, keeps lasting until it is used up. A chat note says which poison went inert.

- **Create Consumable:** the action carries the same kind of crafting rule as PF2e's own Quick Alchemy, so using it opens PF2e's formula picker (the same window as Quick Alchemy: search box, formulas grouped by level), spends one versatile vial, and puts the infused item in a free hand. Its crafting rule leaves out items with the Processed trait (`not item:trait:processed`), which PF2e says take 1 minute to make with Quick Alchemy, so they never appear in the picker. `scripts/alchemist/create-consumable.js` adds the checks around that: it refuses to continue with no versatile vials or no free hand, and in combat it removes the item at the start of the creator's next turn, or when the combat ends. Outside combat it is removed when the world clock moves on (see Expiry outside combat). The vial count is PF2e's versatile vials resource, tied to the quantity of the Versatile Vial item.
- **Quick Alchemy chooser:** it works on the **Quick Alchemy** action. PF2e's own Quick Alchemy button on the Crafting tab is left alone.

- **Using up bomb vials:** the Volatile Vial, Bomber and Toxicologist vials have PF2e's Consumable trait, so PF2e itself takes one off their quantity whenever you make a Strike with them. A vial that reaches 0 is marked as spent but kept, because the chat card's damage buttons still need the item. `scripts/alchemist/volatile-vial.js` then deletes it a minute after its damage is rolled, or when the combat turn changes if no damage was rolled (a miss, say). Out of combat, a spent vial that is never followed by a damage roll stays at quantity 0 until you delete it.

- **Suppressing a drawback (the Mutagenist Field Vial):** every drawback in the module (the Drawback effect of the Cognitive, Juggernaut, Serene, Quicksilver, Bestial, Theatrical, Drakeheart and Mind Doubling Mutagens, and each Ichthys Mutagen effect, which carries its own) has a `RollOption` rule whose option ends `drawback-suppressed`, and its penalties and weaknesses only apply while that option is off. There is no checkbox on the sheet. `scripts/alchemist/mutagenist-field-vial.js` watches for **Effect: Mutagenist Field Vial** being applied to a character (however it gets there, so using the vial and dragging the effect on both work; its slug is `effect-mutagenist-field-vial`). It finds the character's drawback effects that are not already suppressed. With one, it suppresses it without asking; with several, it asks which (a list of the drawbacks with their icons and a short description); with none, it says so in chat and does nothing else. Suppressing sets that rule's `value` to true, adds " (suppressed)" to the drawback effect's name in the effects list, swaps its picture for a suppressed one (the mutagen's own picture greyed out and dimmed, with one long thin red slash from the bottom left to the top right, so it shows at a glance in the effects panel and on the token), and records the effect's id in a flag on the vial's effect and the drawback's own picture in a flag on the drawback. The suppressed pictures are `marquiste-additions/images/suppressed/<name of the picture>.png`, one for each picture a drawback uses; `node foundry/make-suppressed-icons.cjs` makes them from the drawbacks' pictures (it needs the `sharp` package and a Foundry install, and should be run again when a mutagen drawback gets a new picture). A drawback whose picture has no suppressed version keeps its own. When the vial's effect is deleted, whether it ran out or was removed (the active GM's client handles it, or the removing user's if no GM is on), the value goes back to false and the name and picture are restored. The drawback is never removed, so nothing is dealt or applied twice. Only these drawbacks can be suppressed: PF2e's own mutagens have a single combined effect with no drawback of their own. A second vial offers only the drawbacks not already suppressed, and each vial gives back only its own. A mutagen effect already on a character from before this change keeps its old checkbox, which still works, until it is removed and added again from the compendium.

- **Quick Vial:** it finds the research field from the character's Bomber, Chirurgeon, Mutagenist or Toxicologist class feature. It checks that the character has a free hand (using PF2e's own hands-free count) before creating a vial and creates nothing if they don't; the new vial goes into that hand. Applying a coating needs no free hand. It doesn't check for the alchemist's toolkit. Outside combat nothing removes the vial, so delete it yourself.

- **Vials:** the vials, coatings and Mutagenist vial carry the **Fleeting** trait, which `scripts/common/fleeting-trait.js` adds to PF2e's trait lists. In combat they are removed at the end of the turn they were created in (a vial from Quick Vial by that action's own rule); outside combat nothing removes them. The Field Vials' "only for the matching research field" limit isn't enforced. The Toxic Coating effects add their poison damage to every Strike until the end of the turn, so remove them yourself after the first hit.

- **Insulating Paste:** the effect shows a counter badge starting at 5. PF2e doesn't track this itself, so `scripts/cauldron/insulating-paste.js` (loaded by the module) watches PF2e's "damage taken" chat messages. When the paste's resistance reduced the damage, it posts a chat message with a **Use up one instance of protection** button. Pressing it (the character's owner or a GM) lowers the badge by one and removes the effect at 0. Each message can only be counted once. The prompt is posted from the GM's client, so a GM has to be connected. The duration still runs out on its own.

- **Mutant Physique and Mutant Innervation on the copied mutagens:** PF2e's Mutant Physique feat (8th level) names the Bestial, Juggernaut and Quicksilver mutagens, and Mutant Innervation (14th level) names the Cognitive, Serene and Silvertongue mutagens; Drakeheart is in neither. PF2e gives the Mutant Physique benefits through rules that match its own mutagen effects by name (such as `self:effect:juggernaut-mutagen-lesser`), so they do not apply to the copies, whose effects have different slugs, and PF2e leaves Mutant Innervation as text with no rules. Each copy that a feat names therefore carries the benefit itself: a paragraph in the item and effect descriptions ("Mutant Physique If you have the Mutant Physique feat, ...", the same as the Mind Doubling Mutagen's Mutant Innervation paragraph), and rules in its benefit effect that only apply to a character with the feat (`feat:mutant-physique` or `feat:mutant-innervation` in the predicate, which needs the feat's slug to be the PF2e one). The **Juggernaut Mutagen** gives resistance to all physical damage equal to half the character's level; the **Cognitive Mutagen** gives its item bonus to Deception, Diplomacy, Intimidation, Medicine, Nature, Performance, Religion and Survival checks (the telepathy within 60 feet is text only); the **Serene Mutagen**'s Mutant Innervation benefit and the **Quicksilver Mutagen**'s Mutant Physique benefit are text only; the **Bestial Mutagen** gives the Intimidation bonus itself and gets the claw and jaws die step and deadly d10 from PF2e's feat (see its entry). A mutagen spec gives this as `featBenefit` (see `split-mutagen.js`).
- **Drakeheart Mutagen:** PF2e's Final Surge action says the mutagen's duration ends, but nothing in PF2e does that for you, so delete the effect after using it (the drawback effect is removed with it). The Moderate item keeps PF2e's own, different, flavour line. PF2e's Drakeheart Mutagen and its formula are still in the Alchemist pack's list too; this is a separate set of items, so a character should use one or the other.

- **Ichthys Mutagen:** "Breathe water" and "can't breathe air" have no rule element, so they stay as description text. The "Razor Claw" Strike is added next to the character's existing unarmed attacks rather than replacing them (the same way PF2e's Bestial Mutagen adds claws and jaws). The Perception and Wisdom-skill bonus only applies while the **Underwater** toggle on the effect is switched on. The swim Speed uses `@actor.attributes.speed.value`; if it shows 0 on a sheet, check that formula first.

- Mental and emotion saves use the predicate `item:trait:mental` or `item:trait:emotion`, the same pattern PF2e's own effects use. A save against something that isn't tagged with those traits won't get the bonus.
- "Intelligence-based skill checks" is expressed as the skills Arcana, Crafting, Lore, Occultism and Society, plus Perception.
- The re-roll needs the character to own a feat with the slug `mutant-innervation`. If the system names that feat differently, edit the `RollTwice` predicate in `build.js`.
- The item art is the site's image on a square black background. Foundry shows it as the item and effect icon.

## Source traits and the Source line

Every item from these modules says where it came from in two ways, so none of them needs a tag in its name:
- A **source trait** chip: **Marquiste** on the items in Marquiste Additions and **Cauldron** on the items in CorpseCap's Cauldron. They are labels only, with a hover description ("Source: Marquiste Additions. This item is homebrew from ryanmarquiste.github.io/pf2e-remastered-site, not an official PF2e rule."). `scripts/common/source-traits.js` registers them (the Cauldron requires Marquiste Additions, so both live there) and `styles/source-traits.css` gives both one look, a teal chip with a dashed border and italic text, different from PF2e's own traits. The stylesheet matches PF2e's trait chip markup (`.tag[data-trait="marquiste"]`), so if a window draws its traits differently the chip may show in the normal color but is still there.
- A **Source line** at the bottom of the item's description, after a rule, such as "Source Marquiste Additions". It comes from the item's publication title, and it sits at the bottom so the source chip, not the text, is the prominent label. (Food Fight adds its note to a Food Projectile before that line.)

`build.js` adds both to every item except effects, which are only granted by another item. To change them, edit `source-traits.js` and the stylesheet, or the publication titles in `build.js`, then run `node foundry/build.js` and recompile the packs. No item carries a tag in its name any more. The mutagens copied from PF2e (Drakeheart, Cognitive, Juggernaut, Serene, Quicksilver, Bestial and Theatrical) share their names with PF2e's, so their slugs and their formulas' slugs keep a "marquiste" to stay apart from PF2e's.

## Global tags

`foundry/tags.js` holds the name of each package in a long and a short form, for item, feat and ability names:

| Package | Long | Short |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marquiste (Marquiste Additions and the homebrew in general) | Marquiste | (M) |
| CorpseCap's Cauldron | CorpseCap's Cauldron | (CCC) |

`build.js` and `vials.js` build names from them (the short tags are not used in item names now), plus the Cauldron's publication title and labels. To rename a package or change its short form, edit `tags.js` and run `node foundry/build.js`. The scripts in `marquiste-additions/scripts/` are not built and cannot read the tags; the one name they look for, "Quick Alchemy (the Marquiste action)" in `scripts/alchemist/quick-alchemy.js`, has to be changed there by hand.

## Changing or adding items

Edit `build.js`, run `node foundry/build.js`, then repeat step 1 (compile) and replace the module in your Foundry data folder. Item IDs are derived from the item names, so links between the items stay stable across rebuilds.
