  <p><strong>Frequency</strong> at will; <strong>Trigger</strong> You use your instrument as part of a Performance check or as part of casting a composition; <strong>Duration</strong> 10 minutes</p>
  <p>You infuse the instrument with a measure of your magic, bringing it to life. It rises from your hands and hovers in your space, following you as you move so that it always remains in your space, and continues to play at your direction. While it remains in your space, you can cast compositions and use Performance-based abilities as though you were playing it, without holding it or using your hands. It uses your Performance modifier and spellcasting statistics, and it can't act on its own.</p>
  <p>The instrument is an attended item. It can be targeted as though you were holding it, and if it would be taken from you or destroyed, the effect ends. You can Dismiss the effect, and you can use this feat again once it ends.</p>
