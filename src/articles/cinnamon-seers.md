  <h4 class="stat-block-title">Cinnamon Seers</h4>
  <div class="stat-block-traits">

    <span class="trait">Consumable</span>
    <span class="trait">Lozenge</span>

  <p><strong>Usage</strong> held in 1 hand; <strong>Bulk</strong> L<br>
  <strong>Activate</strong> {% actionIcon "one" %} Manipulate</p>
  <p>Zippy, alchemically treated cinnamon suffuses cinnamon seers, a rock candy with a lively taste that provides a mental boost. The Moderate cinnamon seer is the one from the Pathfinder Treasure Vault, and the Lesser is a weaker, shorter-lasting version of it. A cinnamon seer remains in your mouth, its stimulating flavor granting you a +1 item bonus to checks to Recall Knowledge.</p>
  <p><strong>Secondary Effect</strong> {% actionIcon "reaction" %} (fortune)<br>
  <strong>Trigger</strong> You gain no information from a Recall Knowledge check</p>
  <p><strong>Effect</strong> Bite the cinnamon seer and reroll the triggering check. If this was a secret check, the GM rerolls rather than you; the candy doesn't give you any insight into what the GM rolled, so in that case you're rerolling based only on guesswork. The seer becomes inert, and its bonus ends. You become temporarily immune to cinnamon seers until the next time you make your daily preparations.</p>

  <div class="stat-block-section"><span>Lesser</span><span>Item 2</span></div>
  <p><strong>Price</strong> 7 gp</p>
  <p>The seer stays in your mouth for 10 minutes.</p>

  <div class="stat-block-section"><span>Moderate</span><span>Item 4</span></div>
  <p><strong>Price</strong> 14 gp</p>
  <p>The seer stays in your mouth for 1 hour.</p>
</div>
