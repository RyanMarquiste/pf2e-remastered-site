<div class="stat-block" id="cinnamon-seers">
  <h4 class="stat-block-title">Cinnamon Seers</h4>
  <div class="stat-block-traits">
    <span class="trait">Alchemical</span>
    <span class="trait">Consumable</span>
    <span class="trait">Lozenge</span>
  </div>
  <p><strong>Usage</strong> held in 1 hand; <strong>Bulk</strong> L<br>
  <strong>Activate</strong> {% actionIcon "one" %} Manipulate</p>
  <p>Zippy, alchemically treated cinnamon suffuses cinnamon seers, a rock candy with a lively taste that provides a mental boost. The Moderate cinnamon seer is the one from the Pathfinder Treasure Vault, the Lesser is a weaker, shorter-lasting version of it, and the Greater is a stronger one. A cinnamon seer remains in your mouth, its stimulating flavor granting you a +1 item bonus to checks to Recall Knowledge.</p>
  <p><strong>Secondary Effect</strong> {% actionIcon "reaction" %} (fortune)<br>
  <strong>Trigger</strong> You gain no information from a Recall Knowledge check</p>
  <p><strong>Effect</strong> Bite the cinnamon seer and reroll the triggering check (a Lesser rerolls only a check with the skill you chose). If this was a secret check, the GM rerolls rather than you; the candy doesn't give you any insight into what the GM rolled, so in that case you're rerolling based only on guesswork. The seer becomes inert, and its bonus ends. You become temporarily immune to cinnamon seers until the next time you make your daily preparations.</p>

  <div class="stat-block-section"><span>Lesser</span><span>Item 1</span></div>
  <p><strong>Price</strong> 3 gp</p>
  <p>The seer stays in your mouth for 10 minutes. When you activate it, choose Arcana, Nature, Occultism, Religion or Lore. The +1 item bonus applies only to Recall Knowledge checks with that skill, and Bite can reroll only a check with that skill.</p>

  <div class="stat-block-section"><span>Moderate</span><span>Item 4</span></div>
  <p><strong>Price</strong> 14 gp</p>
  <p>The seer stays in your mouth for 1 hour.</p>

  <div class="stat-block-section"><span>Greater</span><span>Item 8</span></div>
  <p><strong>Price</strong> 60 gp</p>
  <p>The seer stays in your mouth for 1 hour, and the item bonus is +2 to Recall Knowledge checks with any skill, Society, Crafting and Medicine included.</p>
</div>

<p>A cinnamon seer is an alchemical food, so a <a href="{{ 'wandering-chef-dedication' | articleUrl(collections.articles) | url }}">Wandering Chef</a> can make it with Quick Cooking.</p>
