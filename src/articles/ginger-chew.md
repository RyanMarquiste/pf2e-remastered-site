  <p><strong>Usage</strong> held in 1 hand; <strong>Bulk</strong> L<br>
  <strong>Activate</strong> {% actionIcon "one" %} Manipulate</p>
  <p>A chewy ginger candy that aids digestion and settles an unsettled stomach. The Moderate ginger chew is the one from the Pathfinder Treasure Vault, and the Lesser is a weaker, shorter-lasting version of it. While it is in your mouth, a ginger chew grants you a +1 item bonus to Fortitude saves against being sickened.</p>
  <p><strong>Secondary Effect</strong> {% actionIcon "one" %}<br>
  <strong>Effect</strong> Bite the ginger chew. Reduce your sickened value by 1, if any. The chew becomes inert, and its bonus ends.</p>

  <div class="stat-block-section"><span>Lesser</span><span>Item 2</span></div>
  <p><strong>Price</strong> 7 gp</p>
  <p>The bonus lasts for 1 minute.</p>

  <div class="stat-block-section"><span>Moderate</span><span>Item 5</span></div>
  <p><strong>Price</strong> 28 gp</p>
  <p>The bonus lasts for 1 hour.</p>
</div>

<p>A ginger chew is an alchemical food, so a <a href="{{ 'wandering-chef-dedication' | articleUrl(collections.articles) | url }}">Wandering Chef</a> can make it with Quick Cooking. The Lesser is also the common level 2 food that makes up the fourth formula in <a href="{{ 'alchemical-cooking' | articleUrl(collections.articles) | url }}">Alchemical Cooking</a>.</p>
