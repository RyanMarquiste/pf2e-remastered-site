  <p><strong>Cast</strong> {% actionIcon "two" %}; <strong>Range</strong> 30 feet; <strong>Targets</strong> 1 creature, or 2 adjacent creatures</p>
  <p>You emit a sound by playing an instrument, vocalizing, or drawing on a sound from your surroundings. The magic of the spell snatches the sound inside a minuscule reflective orb. The sound bounces thousands of times off the inner walls of the orb, the magic amplifying its pitch and loudness. Finally, as the spell concludes, the sound rips into opposing waves that tear across the space and ground themselves in the body of the target, reverberating through them in bone-shattering waves.</p>
  <p>Make a single spell attack roll against the Reflex DC of each target. Direct both waves at one creature, or one wave at each of two adjacent creatures. Compare the attack roll to each target's Reflex DC separately; each wave that hits deals 1d4 sonic damage, doubled on a critical hit. If both waves hit the same creature, combine them into a single damage roll. A creature that is critically hit must succeed at a Fortitude save against your spell DC or be stupefied 1 for 1 round, the discord scrambling its focus.</p>
  <p><strong>Heightened (+2)</strong> The spell deals an additional 2d4 sonic damage (1d4 per wave).</p>
</div>
