  <h4 class="stat-block-title">Pitch Fork <span class="stat-block-action">Cantrip 1</span></h4>
  <div class="stat-block-traits">
    <span class="trait">Cantrip</span>
    <span class="trait">Concentrate</span>
    <span class="trait">Occult</span>
    <span class="trait">Sonic</span>
  </div>
  <div class="stat-block appearance-block">
    <p class="appearance-label">Appearance</p>
    <p>A sound is snatched inside a minuscule reflective orb, bouncing thousands of times off its inner walls as its pitch and loudness build. Finally, the sound rips into opposing waves that tear across the space and ground themselves in the body of the target, reverberating through them in bone-shattering waves.</p>
  </div>
  <p><strong>Cast</strong> {% actionIcon "two" %}; <strong>Range</strong> 30 feet</p>
  <p><strong>Targets</strong> 1 or 2 creatures adjacent to each other</p>
  <p><strong>Attack</strong> your spell attack modifier (single roll) against Reflex DC (compared against each target)</p>
  <p><strong>Damage</strong> 2d4 sonic damage to each target hit plus, on a critical hit, the target is stupefied 1 for 1 round</p>
  <p>You emit a sound by playing an instrument, vocalizing, or drawing on a sound from your surroundings.</p>
  <p><strong>Heightened (+1)</strong> The spell deals an additional 1d4 sonic damage.</p>
</div>
