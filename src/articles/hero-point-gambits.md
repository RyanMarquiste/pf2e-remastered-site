Hero Points in the Remastered rules are great at preventing a bad dying roll, but they rarely come up outside of combat. **Hero Point Gambits** give players a proactive way to spend them.

## The Rule

Once per encounter, a player may spend **1 Hero Point** *before* rolling a check to declare a Gambit:

> "I'm certain this works, because [reason tied to my character]."

If the reason is accepted by the GM as reasonable, the player rolls the check with a +2 circumstance bonus. If the check fails anyway, the fiction plays out as normal — the Gambit only affects the roll, not the consequences of failure.

## Why It Works

- It keeps Hero Points relevant in scenes outside of near-death saves.
- It rewards players for grounding actions in their character's backstory or class fantasy.
- The GM retains a soft veto: if the justification doesn't fit the fiction, the Gambit isn't allowed that turn.

## Table Notes

This is intentionally a small bonus (+2, matching the existing circumstance bonus tier) so it doesn't compete with existing Hero Point uses like rerolling or stabilizing. Consider capping it to once per encounter per character to keep spending meaningful.
