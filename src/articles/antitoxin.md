<div class="stat-block" id="antitoxin">
  <h4 class="stat-block-title">Antitoxin</h4>
  <p>This item or effect counteracts a specific poison, rather than harming the creature that consumes it. An item with this trait is not a poison, even if it was created from one, and isn't affected by abilities that interact with poisons.</p>
  <p>When a creature drinks an item with this trait, it becomes immune to the specific poison the item was made to counter until the end of its next long rest. A creature can be under the effect of only one antitoxin at a time; if it would come under the effect of a second, the first ends immediately.</p>
</div>
<p class="trait-note"><strong>Special</strong> The Antitoxin trait exists to clearly flag when an item has been converted from a poison into a targeted countermeasure against that same poison, since it changes the item's purpose entirely and should no longer be treated as a poison by anything that keys off that trait.</p>
