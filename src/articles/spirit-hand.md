  <p><strong>Range</strong> 30 feet; <strong>Duration</strong> 1 minute</p>
  <p>You sever a piece of your own spirit at the wrist, creating a duplicate of your hand within range. You can make it appear as a translucent, ghostly shape or render it invisible, as you prefer.</p>
  <p>The hand has AC equal to your unarmored proficiency AC and 5 Hit Points. It's spectral, and can't be damaged except by weapons or effects with the ghost touch trait. As a Concentrate action, you can move the hand up to your Speed, as long as it remains within range. The hand can perform any Interact action you could normally perform with a hand &mdash; retrieving an item, operating a device, opening a container, and so on &mdash; and can pass through solid barriers such as walls and doors, as normal for an incorporeal effect &mdash; it can't pass through barriers made of force, and it still can't move beyond the spell's range. It can't make a Strike or use any action with the attack trait.</p>
  <p>As an Activate action, if the hand is touching a creature, you can destroy it, converting its remaining Hit Points into an equal amount of healing for that creature.</p>
  <p>If the hand is destroyed by any means, the spell ends immediately and you take damage equal to the hand's maximum Hit Points.</p>
  <p><strong>Heightened (+1)</strong> The hand's Hit Points increase by 2.</p>
</div>
