<div class="stat-block" id="adhesive-fluids">
  <h4 class="stat-block-title">Adhesive Fluids <span class="stat-block-action">Feat 2</span></h4>
  <div class="stat-block-traits">
    <span class="trait">Alchemist</span>
    <span class="trait">Additive</span>
  </div>
  <p><strong>Trigger</strong> You use Quick Alchemy to create a bomb with the splash trait.</p>
  <p>You work a sticky resin into the bomb so that it clings to whatever it strikes. For the Strike you make with this bomb, the creature you target takes extra damage from the bomb's splash as if it had a weakness to splash damage. The weakness is equal to the bomb's splash damage, before any status or other bonuses are applied, plus 2 for each size category the target is larger than Medium.</p>
  <p>A Large creature is one size category larger than Medium, a Huge creature two and a Gargantuan creature three. A creature of Medium size or smaller has no size increase, but still has the weakness equal to the bomb's splash damage. For example, a Volatile Vial that deals 3 splash damage gives its target weakness 3 to splash damage if it is Medium, 5 if it is Large, 7 if it is Huge and 9 if it is Gargantuan.</p>
  <p>The weakness applies to the target of your Strike only, not to any other creature in the splash area, and it applies to the splash damage the target takes even if your Strike misses. As with any weakness, it is not increased on a critical hit. If the target already has a weakness to splash damage, use whichever is higher instead of adding them together.</p>
  <div class="stat-block special-block">
    <p class="special-label">Special</p>
    <p>Only one additive can be applied to each bomb you create with Quick Alchemy, but there is no limit on how often you can use Adhesive Fluids: you can add it to every bomb you create.</p>
  </div>
</div>
