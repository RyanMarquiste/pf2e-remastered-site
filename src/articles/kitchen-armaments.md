<div class="stat-block" id="kitchen-armaments">
  <h4 class="stat-block-title">Kitchen Armaments <span class="stat-block-action">Feat 4</span></h4>
  <div class="stat-block-traits">
    <span class="trait">Archetype</span>
  </div>
  <p><strong>Prerequisites</strong> Wandering Chef Dedication</p>
  <p>You have spent enough time in a kitchen to know which tools make good weapons. You become trained in the <a href="{{ 'chefs-knife' | articleUrl(collections.articles) | url }}">chef's knife</a>, <a href="{{ 'cleaver' | articleUrl(collections.articles) | url }}">cleaver</a>, <a href="{{ 'tenderizer' | articleUrl(collections.articles) | url }}">tenderizer</a>, <a href="{{ 'skewer' | articleUrl(collections.articles) | url }}">skewer</a>, <a href="{{ 'rolling-pin' | articleUrl(collections.articles) | url }}">rolling pin</a>, and frying pan. Whenever you gain a class feature that grants you expert or higher proficiency in simple weapons, you also gain that rank in these weapons.</p>
  <p>Once your proficiency rank in one of these weapons is expert or higher, you gain access to its critical specialization effect.</p>
