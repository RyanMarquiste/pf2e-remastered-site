  <p><strong>Usage</strong> held in 1 hand; <strong>Bulk</strong> L<br>
  <strong>Activate</strong> {% actionIcon "one" %} Interact</p>
  <p>This vial holds a pungent, sulphur-yellow slurry that reeks of struck matches and rotten eggs. While under the effects of this mutagen, your skin weeps volatile sulphur compounds that you can exhale as a thick yellow cloud.</p>
  <p>You gain resistance to fire, scaled by tier below, as the compounds in your blood soak up heat.</p>
  <p><strong>Activate</strong> {% actionIcon "one" %} Sulphur Cloud</p>
  <p>You exhale a cloud of sulphur fumes that fills a 5-foot emanation around you (10 feet at Greater and Major). The cloud stays where it forms, so you can move out of it, and it disperses at the start of your next turn. Only one cloud can exist at a time; creating a new one disperses the previous one. The cloud has no effect on its own.</p>
  <p><strong>Ignition</strong> If a creature or object in the cloud takes fire or electricity damage, or an attack or effect with the fire or electricity trait is made into or through the cloud, the cloud ignites, as sparks and flame both set sulphur off. Any source of fire or electricity counts, including your enemies', and readying a fire or electricity attack for when a creature enters the cloud is a classic way to spring the trap. When the cloud ignites, each creature in it takes fire damage as noted below for this mutagen's tier, and the cloud disperses. Creatures other than you attempt a basic Reflex save against the item's DC; you attempt none, as your fire resistance from this mutagen always covers the damage. It affects allies and enemies alike.</p>
  <p><strong>Drawback</strong> The compounds building up in your body are easily overpowered by caustic and toxic substances, giving you weakness 3 to acid and weakness 3 to poison. Sulphur also tarnishes and corrodes metal, giving you a &minus;1 item penalty to attack and damage rolls with metal weapons and a &minus;1 item penalty to AC while you wear metal armor or wield a metal shield (this doesn't strip special material properties, such as silver). The odorous, tear-inducing vapors that cling to you make social graces difficult, giving you a &minus;2 item penalty to Charisma-based skill checks. The reek is also easy to follow: creatures attempting to track you by scent gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Survival checks to Track you and to Perception checks to notice you by smell.</p>
