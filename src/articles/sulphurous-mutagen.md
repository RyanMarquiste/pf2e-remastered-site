  <p><strong>Usage</strong> held in 1 hand; <strong>Bulk</strong> L<br>
  <strong>Activate</strong> {% actionIcon "one" %} Interact</p>
  <p>This vial holds a pungent, sulphur-yellow slurry that reeks of struck matches and rotten eggs. While under the effects of this mutagen, your skin weeps volatile sulphur compounds that gather around you as a faint yellow haze.</p>
  <p>You gain resistance to fire, scaled by tier below, as the compounds in your blood soak up heat.</p>
  <p><strong>Aura</strong> 5 feet (10 feet at Greater and Major)</p>
  <p>A cloud of sulphur fumes surrounds you. The cloud has no effect on its own. If fire damage is dealt to a creature in the cloud, or an effect with the fire trait passes through it, the cloud ignites: each creature in the cloud takes fire damage as noted below for this mutagen's tier, and the cloud disperses. Creatures other than you attempt a basic Reflex save against the item's DC; you attempt none, and your fire resistance negates the damage. The cloud reforms at the start of your next turn. It affects allies and enemies alike, and any source of fire can set it off, including your enemies'.</p>
  <p><strong>Drawback</strong> The compounds building up in your body are easily overpowered by caustic and toxic substances, giving you weakness 3 to acid and weakness 3 to poison. The odorous, tear-inducing vapors that cling to you make social graces difficult, giving you a &minus;2 item penalty to Charisma-based skill checks. The reek is also easy to follow: creatures attempting to track you by scent gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Survival checks to Track you and to Perception checks to notice you by smell.</p>
