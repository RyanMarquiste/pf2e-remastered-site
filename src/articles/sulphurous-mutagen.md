  <p><strong>Usage</strong> held in 1 hand; <strong>Bulk</strong> L<br>
  <strong>Activate</strong> {% actionIcon "one" %} Interact</p>
  <p>This vial holds a pungent, sulphur-yellow slurry that reeks of struck matches and rotten eggs. While under the effects of this mutagen, your skin weeps volatile sulphur compounds that gather around you as a faint yellow haze.</p>
  <p>You gain resistance to fire equal to half this mutagen's level (minimum 1), as the compounds in your blood soak up heat.</p>
  <p><strong>Aura</strong> 5 feet</p>
  <p>A cloud of sulphur fumes surrounds you. The cloud has no effect on its own. If fire damage is dealt to a creature in the cloud, or an effect with the fire trait passes through it, the cloud ignites: each creature in the cloud, including you, takes fire damage as noted below for this mutagen's tier, with a basic Reflex save against the item's DC, and the cloud disperses. The cloud reforms at the start of your next turn. This affects allies and enemies alike, and any source of fire can set it off, including your enemies'.</p>
  <p><strong>Drawback</strong> The compounds building up in your body are easily overpowered by caustic and toxic substances. You have weakness 5 to acid and weakness 5 to poison.</p>
