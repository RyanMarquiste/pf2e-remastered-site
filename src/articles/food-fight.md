<div class="stat-block" id="food-fight">
  <h4 class="stat-block-title">Food Fight <span class="stat-block-action">Feat 6</span></h4>
  <div class="stat-block-traits">
    <span class="trait">Archetype</span>
  </div>
  <p><strong>Prerequisites</strong> Wandering Chef Dedication, <a href="{{ 'flash-cook' | articleUrl(collections.articles) | url }}">Flash Cook</a></p>
  <p>You have learned to turn whatever is on the table into a weapon, and to throw it the moment it leaves the pan.</p>
  <p><strong>Flash Cook and Strike</strong> When you use Flash Cook, you can Strike with the Food Projectile it creates as part of the same action. This Strike counts toward your multiple attack penalty as normal.</p>
  <p><strong>Deadlier and farther</strong> The Food Projectiles you create with Flash Cook gain the deadly d8 trait, so on a critical hit they deal an extra d8 damage after the damage is doubled. Their range also increases from 10 feet to 15 feet.</p>
</div>
