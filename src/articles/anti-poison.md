<div class="stat-block" id="anti-poison">
  <h4 class="stat-block-title">Anti-Poison <span class="stat-block-action">Feat 4</span></h4>
  <div class="stat-block-traits">
    <span class="trait">Alchemist</span>
    <span class="trait">Poisoner</span>
  </div>
  <p>When you create an item that duplicates a named poison using Advanced Alchemy or Quick Alchemy, you can instead create its antitoxin version. The resulting item gains the antitoxin trait, loses the poison trait, and no longer functions as a poison. When a creature drinks it, that creature becomes immune to the specific poison it was made from until the end of its next long rest.</p>
  <p><strong>Special</strong> A creature can be under the effect of only one antitoxin at a time; if it would come under the effect of a second, the first ends. This can only be applied to a named poison with its own stat block &mdash; it has no effect on generic poison damage or persistent poison damage.</p>
</div>
