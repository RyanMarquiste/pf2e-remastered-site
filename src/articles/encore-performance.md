  <h4 class="stat-block-title">Encore Performance <span class="stat-block-action">{% actionIcon "free" %} Feature 3</span></h4>
  <div class="stat-block-traits">

  <p><strong>Frequency</strong> at will; <strong>Trigger</strong> You perform with your instrument, voice, or body as part of a Performance check or as part of casting a composition; <strong>Duration</strong> 10 minutes</p>
  <p>You infuse your performance with a measure of your magic, bringing it to life. A nonsubstantial visual figment of yourself separates from you and takes up the performance, taking any instrument, script, or prop you were using. It follows you as you move, always remaining in your space, and continues to perform at your direction. It can only make Performance checks and cast compositions, using your Performance modifier and spellcasting statistics as though you were performing yourself. It can't speak, take other actions, or produce any other effects, and it has no actions of its own.</p>
  <p>The figment is easy to distinguish as a visual effect and provides no combat or movement benefits. It doesn't block movement or vision, and creatures and objects pass through it unaffected.</p>
  <p>It is bound by the same limits you are. While you couldn't perform that way yourself (for example, if you are silenced), it can't either. You can Dismiss the effect, and you can use this feature again once it ends.</p>
  <p>Choose the option that matches the type of performance you used to trigger this feature: playing an instrument, orating or singing, dancing, or acting or performing comedy.</p>
  <p><strong>Play an Instrument</strong> The figment plays your instrument, whether strings, keys, percussion, or winds, freeing your hands.</p>
  <p><strong>Orate or Sing</strong> The figment sings or orates, accompanied by an echo of your voice that swirls out from your throat. If you read from a script, scroll, or book, the figment holds it.</p>
  <p><strong>Dance</strong> The figment dances or performs other visual performances. It can twirl any prop you use, such as a ribbon, fan, or hoop.</p>
  <p><strong>Act or Perform Comedy</strong> The figment acts out your scene, drama, pantomime, puppetry, or comic bit, accompanied by an echo of your voice. It can wear or work any prop you use, such as a mask or puppet.</p>
  <p><strong>Limits</strong> An instrument's bonus to Performance checks applies to the Performance checks and compositions the figment performs. Any other ability of an instrument, script, or prop, including Activate abilities and effects that apply while you are playing or holding it, has no effect while the figment holds the item. You can use those abilities only while you are holding the item yourself.</p>
  <p><strong>Special</strong> An instrument, script, or prop held by the figment is an attended item. It can be targeted as though you were holding it, and if it is taken from you or destroyed, the effect ends. The figment and the echo aren't objects and can't be targeted.</p>
