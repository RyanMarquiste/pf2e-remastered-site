  <h4 class="stat-block-title">Encore Performance <span class="stat-block-action">{% actionIcon "free" %} Feature 3</span></h4>
  <div class="stat-block-traits">
    <span class="trait">Bard</span>
    <span class="trait">Concentrate</span>
  </div>
  <p><strong>Frequency</strong> at will; <strong>Trigger</strong> You perform with your instrument, voice, or body as part of a Performance check or as part of casting a composition; <strong>Duration</strong> 10 minutes</p>
  <p>You infuse your performance with a measure of your magic, bringing it to life. It follows you as you move, always remaining in your space, and continues to perform at your direction. It can only make Performance checks and cast compositions, using your Performance modifier and spellcasting statistics as though you were performing yourself. It can't speak, take other actions, or produce any other effects, and it has no actions of its own.</p>
  <p>It is bound by the same limits you are. While you couldn't perform that way yourself (for example, if you are silenced), it can't either. You can Dismiss the effect, and you can use this feature again once it ends.</p>
  <p>Choose the option that matches the type of performance you used to trigger this feature: playing an instrument, orating or singing, dancing, or acting or performing comedy.</p>
  <p><strong>Play an Instrument</strong> Your instrument, whether strings, keys, percussion, or winds, rises from your hands and hovers in your space, playing at your direction and freeing your hands.</p>
  <p><strong>Orate or Sing</strong> An echo of your voice swirls out from your throat and dances around you as you sing or orate. If you read from a script, scroll, or book, it can hover beside you for the echo to read from.</p>
  <p><strong>Dance</strong> A nonsubstantial visual figment of yourself separates from you, performing your dance or other visual performances. It can twirl any prop you use, such as a ribbon, fan, or hoop, which hovers with it.</p>
  <p><strong>Act or Perform Comedy</strong> A nonsubstantial visual figment of yourself separates from you and acts out your scene, drama, pantomime, puppetry, or comic bit, accompanied by an echo of your voice. It can wear or work any prop you use, such as a mask or puppet, which hovers with it.</p>
  <p>A figment is easy to distinguish as a visual effect and provides no combat or movement benefits. It doesn't block movement or vision, and creatures and objects pass through it unaffected.</p>
  <p><strong>Limits</strong> An instrument counts as played only while you spend an action performing with it. Effects that apply "while playing" or "while holding" an instrument don't apply the rest of the time. The hovering instrument, echo, and figment can't use Activate abilities; abilities of an instrument, script, or prop that require an Activate action can be used only while you are holding the item yourself.</p>
  <p><strong>Special</strong> An instrument, script, or prop that hovers with this feature is an attended item. It can be targeted as though you were holding it, and if it is taken from you or destroyed, the effect ends. The echo and the figment aren't objects and can't be targeted.</p>
