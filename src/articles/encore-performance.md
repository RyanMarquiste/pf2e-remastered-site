  <h4 class="stat-block-title">Encore Rune <span class="stat-block-action">Item 2</span></h4>
  <div class="stat-block-traits">
    <span class="trait">Magical</span>
    <span class="trait">Rune</span>
  </div>
  <div class="stat-block appearance-block">
    <p class="appearance-label">Appearance</p>
    <p>A small silver glyph of looping notes, etched into the tool, that glimmers faintly whenever the tool is played.</p>
  </div>
  <p><strong>Price</strong> 35 gp; <strong>Usage</strong> etched onto a performance tool; <strong>Bulk</strong> &mdash;</p>
  <p>This rune can be etched onto any object used in performing, such as a musical instrument, baton, puppet, or fan. It benefits tools that tie up your hands the most. This rune doesn't use any of the item's rune slots, and doesn't stop the tool from having its own item bonus or other magical properties.</p>
  <p><strong>Duration</strong> sustained (through a Performance skill check) each round for up to 1 minute</p>
  <p>When you use Performance with the tool, such as playing the instrument, waving the baton, working the puppet, or twirling the fan, you may choose to animate the tool. If you do, it comes to life and continues to perform using your Performance skill, so your hands are free for other things.</p>
  <p>You Sustain the effect by taking a Performance check action through the tool, which you can do without holding it, so the tool doesn't need to return to your hand. This doesn't use the Sustain action and doesn't count as Sustaining a spell. Each time you Sustain it, the effect lasts until the end of your next turn. If you don't, it ends at the end of your next turn.</p>
  <p>The animated tool is considered worn by you (not an unattended item). Enemies can attack it the same way they could attack an item you are wearing.</p>
  <p>The tool stays in your space while it performs, and it moves with you if you move.</p>
  <p>While the tool isn't actively held in a hand, only Performance checks can be made through it, and you can make them without taking the tool into your hand. Activating any other effect of the tool requires it to be held in a hand.</p>
  <p>The tool can't Strike, make attacks, or supply defenses unless it is held in a hand.</p>
  <p>When the effect ends, the tool returns to your hands if you have enough free hands to take it. If you don't, it drops in the space you are in.</p>
</div>
