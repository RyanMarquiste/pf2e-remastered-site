  <h4 class="stat-block-title">Morning Side Dishes <span class="stat-block-action">Feat 4</span></h4>
  <div class="stat-block-traits">
    <span class="trait">Archetype</span>
  </div>
  <p><strong>Source</strong> Pathfinder Lost Omens: Tian Xia Character Guide pg. 100</p>
  <p><strong>Prerequisites</strong> <a href="{{ 'wandering-chef-dedication' | articleUrl(collections.articles) | url }}">Wandering Chef Dedication</a></p>
  <p>You know a proper breakfast should have a variety of sides. You gain the Advanced Cooking feature, so that you can prepare 5 alchemical foods during your daily preparations.</p>

  <div class="stat-block-section" id="advanced-cooking"><span>Advanced Cooking</span><span>Feature</span></div>
  <p>During your daily preparations, you spend some time to prepare alchemical foods that you can eat over the course of the day. You don't need to attempt a Crafting check to do this, you can use a <a href="{{ 'cooking-toolkit' | articleUrl(collections.articles) | url }}">cooking toolkit</a> instead of an alchemist's lab, and you ignore both the number of days typically required to make the foods and any raw material requirements.</p>
  <p>You can prepare up to 5 alchemical foods. Each must be a food you know the formula for and have an item level equal to or lower than your level. These foods have the infused trait and remain potent for 24 hours or until your next daily preparations, whichever comes first.</p>
  <p>Advanced Cooking is a section of its own, next to an Alchemist's Advanced Alchemy if you have one, and only alchemical foods can be prepared in it.</p>
