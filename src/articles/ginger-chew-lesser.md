  <p>A small, chewy lozenge of candied ginger that settles an unsettled stomach. It is a weaker, shorter-lasting version of the Ginger Chew from the Pathfinder Treasure Vault. A lesser ginger chew lasts for 1 minute and grants you a +1 item bonus to Fortitude saves against being sickened.</p>
  <p><strong>Secondary Effect</strong> {% actionIcon "one" %}<br>
  <strong>Effect</strong> Reduce your sickened value by 1, if any. The chew becomes inert.</p>
</div>

<p>Ginger Chew (Lesser) is an alchemical food, so a <a href="{{ 'wandering-chef-dedication' | articleUrl(collections.articles) | url }}">Wandering Chef</a> can make it with Quick Cooking. It is also the common level 2 food that makes up the fourth formula in <a href="{{ 'alchemical-cooking' | articleUrl(collections.articles) | url }}">Alchemical Cooking</a>.</p>
