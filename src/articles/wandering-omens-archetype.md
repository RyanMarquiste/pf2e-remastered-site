The **Wandering Omen** archetype is for characters who treat the world itself as a source of guidance — cracked stones, bird flight, the way a candle burns.

## Prerequisites

Trained in Nature, Occultism, Religion, or Society (any one).

## Dedication Feat — Wandering Omen Dedication

**Feat 2, Archetype**

You've learned to read portents in everyday things. You become trained in your choice of an additional skill from Nature, Occultism, Religion, or Society. You gain the **Read the Signs** action.

**Read the Signs** *(concentrate, exploration)*: Spend 10 minutes interpreting omens from your surroundings about a specific upcoming decision or danger. Attempt a check against your chosen skill at a DC set by the GM (typically equal to a level-appropriate DC). On a success, the GM gives you one true, if vague, hint relevant to that decision. On a critical failure, the GM may give you a misleading hint instead.

## Feat 4 — Favorable Portent

**Feat 4, Archetype**

Once per day, after using Read the Signs successfully, you may grant one ally within 30 feet a +1 circumstance bonus to their next skill check or saving throw related to the hint given, as long as they act on it within the next hour.

## Feat 8 — Ill Omen

**Feat 8, Archetype**

You can read bad portents for your enemies too. Once per day, as a single action, choose one enemy you can see. Attempt a check with your Wandering Omen skill against their Will DC. On a success, that enemy takes a -1 status penalty to their next saving throw. This is a divination effect.

## Design Notes

This archetype is intentionally light on combat impact and heavy on giving GMs a built-in, mechanically supported way to feed hints without breaking verisimilitude. It pairs naturally with investigation-focused campaigns.
