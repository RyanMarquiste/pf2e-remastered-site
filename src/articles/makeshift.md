<div class="stat-block" id="makeshift">
  <h4 class="stat-block-title">Makeshift</h4>
  <p>A makeshift item is an everyday object pressed into service as a weapon, shield, or suit of armor. It was never built for the job, so it may break when it is needed most. When it breaks it gains the <a href="{{ 'broken' | articleUrl(collections.articles) | url }}">broken</a> trait. Its Hit Points, if it has any, don't change.</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Weapon</strong> If you roll a critical failure on a Strike with a makeshift weapon, it breaks.</li>
    <li><strong>Shield</strong> If you have a makeshift shield raised and an attack against you is a critical hit, the shield breaks.</li>
    <li><strong>Armor</strong> If you are critically hit and you don't have a shield raised, your makeshift armor breaks.</li>
  </ul>
  <p>A broken makeshift weapon can't be used to Strike. It stays in your hand, so you must stow it and draw another weapon to keep attacking. A broken makeshift shield or suit of armor works as any broken shield or armor does. A makeshift item with no Hit Points is mended by a successful Repair check, as described under the broken trait.</p>
  <p>If you or an ally rerolls the attack roll (a hero point, or any other fortune effect) and the new result is not a critical failure or critical hit, the item doesn't break.</p>
</div>
