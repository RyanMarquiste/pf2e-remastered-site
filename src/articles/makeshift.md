<div class="stat-block" id="makeshift">
  <h4 class="stat-block-title">Makeshift</h4>
  <p>A makeshift item is an everyday object pressed into service as a weapon, shield, or suit of armor. It was never built for the job, so it may break when it is needed most. The item has the Hardness, Hit Points, and Broken Threshold of the material it is made from, and it becomes broken the way any broken item does.</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Weapon</strong> If you roll a critical failure on a Strike with a makeshift weapon, it breaks.</li>
    <li><strong>Shield</strong> If you have a makeshift shield raised and an attack against you is a critical hit, the shield breaks.</li>
    <li><strong>Armor</strong> If you are critically hit and you don't have a shield raised, your makeshift armor breaks.</li>
  </ul>
  <p>A broken makeshift weapon can't be used to Strike. It stays in your hand, so you must stow it and draw another weapon to keep attacking. A broken makeshift shield or suit of armor works as any broken shield or armor does. A broken makeshift item can be fixed with the Repair action.</p>
  <p>If you or an ally rerolls the attack roll (a hero point, or any other fortune effect) and the new result is not a critical failure or critical hit, the item doesn't break.</p>
</div>
<p class="trait-note"><strong>Special</strong> Weapons don't normally take damage, so the Hardness and Hit Points on a makeshift weapon are there only to say when it breaks and how hard it is to repair. They don't make the weapon harder to hit with or easier to damage.</p>
