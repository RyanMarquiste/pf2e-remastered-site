  <h4 class="stat-block-title">Wandering Chef Dedication <span class="stat-block-action">Feat 2</span></h4>
  <div class="stat-block-traits">
    <span class="trait">Archetype</span>
    <span class="trait">Dedication</span>
  </div>
  <p><strong>Source</strong> Pathfinder Lost Omens: Tian Xia Character Guide pg. 100</p>
  <p>Whether trained in a noble kitchen or a humble home, you know how to cook delicious dishes and source ingredients from the wilderness or urban settings. You become trained in Crafting; if you were already trained in Crafting, you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You can use a <a href="{{ 'cooking-toolkit' | articleUrl(collections.articles) | url }}">cooking toolkit</a> or cookware instead of an alchemist's toolkit to craft alchemical foods. When using the Subsist downtime activity, you can use Crafting or Cooking Lore in place of Survival, and if you roll a failure, you get a success instead.</p>
  <p>You gain the <a href="{{ 'quick-cooking' | articleUrl(collections.articles) | url }}">Quick Cooking</a> action, which works like Quick Alchemy but makes only alchemical food. Any items you choose with Alchemical Crafting must be alchemical food, but they can be 1st level or 2nd level instead of only 1st level.</p>
  <p>You create up to 4 food packets during your daily preparations. Food packets work like versatile vials, but can be used only for Quick Cooking. Typically, a wandering chef's food packets take the form of parcels of foraged ingredients.</p>
