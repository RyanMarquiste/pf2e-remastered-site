  <div class="stat-block-traits">

    <span class="trait">Skill</span>

  <p><strong>Prerequisites</strong> Trained in Crafting</p>
  <p>You can use the Craft activity to create alchemical foods, but not other alchemical items. When you select this feat, you immediately gain the formulas for four common alchemical foods of 1st level or 2nd level, which you choose. If you want to choose an uncommon alchemical food of that level, the GM decides whether you can, such as when the food's Access entry applies to you.</p>
  <p>The <a href="{{ 'wandering-chef-dedication' | articleUrl(collections.articles) | url }}">Wandering Chef Dedication</a> gives you this feat.</p>
