  <h4 class="stat-block-title">Mutant Physique and Mutant Innervation <span class="stat-block-action">Alchemist Feats</span></h4>
  <div class="stat-block-traits">
    <span class="trait">Alchemist</span>
  </div>
  <p><strong>Mutant Physique</strong> (feat 8) and <strong>Mutant Innervation</strong> (feat 14) are alchemist feats that grant an extra benefit while you are under the effects of certain mutagens. As printed, each feat names the specific mutagens it improves, so every new mutagen would otherwise require the feat itself to be updated.</p>
  <p>This site leaves the feats as printed. Instead, each mutagen that works with one of these feats carries its own <strong>feat benefit block</strong> in its description. The block names the feat, states its prerequisite, and lists the extra benefit the mutagen gives to a character with that feat. A new or revised mutagen only has to be changed in one place.</p>

  <div class="stat-block-section"><span>Reading a Feat Benefit Block</span></div>
  <p><strong>Prerequisite</strong> Even though a benefit is listed on the item, it is available only to a character who has taken the feat named in its block. Without the feat, the block does nothing and the rest of the mutagen works as normal. Each block opens with a Prerequisite line so this is never in doubt.</p>
  <p><strong>Which feat</strong> A <strong>Mutant Physique</strong> block appears on physical mutagens, which change the body. A <strong>Mutant Innervation</strong> block appears on mental mutagens, which change the mind, senses, or presence.</p>
  <p><strong>Multiple mutagens</strong> As with the feats themselves, if you can be under the effects of more than one mutagen, such as through the mutagenist greater field discovery, you gain the benefits of every block that applies.</p>
