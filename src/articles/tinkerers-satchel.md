A **Tinkerer's Satchel** is a reinforced bag of components, from tension springs to signal wire, meant for characters who like improvising solutions between encounters.

## Tinkerer's Satchel

**Item 3, Worn Item, Uncommon**

**Price** 60 gp · **Bulk** 1

You gain access to a pool of **3 Component Points**, which refresh after a full night's rest. As an activity with the **manipulate** trait, you may spend Component Points to craft one temporary gadget from the list below. Gadgets last until the end of your next encounter or 10 minutes, whichever is longer, then break down back into parts.

| Gadget | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Signal Line | 1 | Two creatures holding an end can send simple prearranged signals (danger, all clear, come here) across any distance within line of sight. |
| Brace Wedge | 1 | Jam a door or lever in place; requires a DC 18 Athletics check to force open. |
| Sparking Fuse | 2 | Create a small distraction: a loud pop and bright flash in a 5-foot square, as a diversion for Sneaking or Feinting. |
| Pulley Rig | 2 | Rig a simple pulley that halves the Bulk of one object for the purpose of hauling it vertically. |

## Design Notes

This item is meant to reward player creativity outside of combat without stepping on the toes of actual magic items or class abilities. The Component Point pool is deliberately small and per-day to keep it a supplement to problem-solving, not a subsystem you build a whole character around.
