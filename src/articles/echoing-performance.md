  <h4 class="stat-block-title">Echoing Performance <span class="stat-block-action">{% actionIcon "free" %} Feature 3</span></h4>
  <div class="stat-block-traits">
    <span class="trait">Bard</span>
    <span class="trait">Concentrate</span>
  </div>
  <p><strong>Frequency</strong> at will; <strong>Trigger</strong> You use your instrument, voice, or dance as part of a Performance check or as part of casting a composition; <strong>Duration</strong> 10 minutes</p>
  <p>You infuse your performance with a measure of your magic, bringing it to life. It follows you as you move, always remaining in your space, and continues to perform at your direction. It can only make Performance checks and cast compositions, using your Performance modifier and spellcasting statistics as though you were performing yourself. It can't speak, take other actions, or produce any other effects, and it has no actions of its own.</p>
  <p>It is bound by the same limits you are. While you couldn't perform that way yourself (for example, if you are silenced), it can't either. You can Dismiss the effect, and you can use this feature again once it ends.</p>
  <p><strong>Instrument</strong> Your instrument rises from your hands and hovers in your space, playing at your direction and freeing your hands.</p>
  <p><strong>Oratory or Singing</strong> An echo of your voice swirls out from your throat and dances around you as you sing or orate. If you read from a script, scroll, or book, it can hover beside you for the echo to read from.</p>
  <p><strong>Dance</strong> A nonsubstantial visual figment of yourself separates from you, performing your dance or other visual performances. It can twirl any prop you use, such as a ribbon, fan, or hoop, which hovers with it. The figment is easy to distinguish as a visual effect and provides no combat or movement benefits. It doesn't block movement or vision, and creatures and objects pass through it unaffected.</p>
  <p><strong>Special</strong> An instrument, script, or prop that hovers with this feature is an attended item. It can be targeted as though you were holding it, and if it is taken from you or destroyed, the effect ends. The echo and the figment aren't objects and can't be targeted.</p>
</div>
