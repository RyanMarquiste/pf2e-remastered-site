  <h4 class="stat-block-title">Cookbook (Blank) <span class="stat-block-action">Item 0</span></h4>
  <p><strong>Price</strong> 1 gp; <strong>Usage</strong> held in 1 hand; <strong>Bulk</strong> L</p>
  <p>A cookbook is a formula book kept by a cook. It holds the formulas for alchemical foods, and only alchemical foods: a character who wants one book for everything keeps a formula book instead. A cook can keep as many cookbooks as they like, for example one for camp meals and one for feasts. Formulas can also appear on recipe cards, menus, and almost any other medium; there's no need to copy them into a cookbook as long as you can keep them on hand to reference them.</p>
</div>

<p>A cookbook works like a formula book in every other way. The recipes in it are known formulas, so the character can choose them for <a href="{{ 'quick-cooking' | articleUrl(collections.articles) | url }}">Quick Cooking</a> and for the <a href="{{ 'morning-side-dishes' | articleUrl(collections.articles) | url }}">Advanced Cooking</a> they prepare each day.</p>
