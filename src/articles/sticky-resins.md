<div class="stat-block" id="sticky-resins">
  <h4 class="stat-block-title">Sticky Resins <span class="stat-block-action">Feat 2</span></h4>
  <div class="stat-block-traits">
    <span class="trait">Alchemist</span>
    <span class="trait">Additive</span>
  </div>
  <p><strong>Trigger</strong> You use Quick Alchemy to create a bomb with the splash trait.</p>
  <p>Using a sticky resin additive, you cause your alchemical bomb's splash to coat and stick to your target on a successful attack. If your Strike with the bomb is a success or a critical success, the target temporarily gains a weakness to splash damage, lasting until the end of your turn, as the splash coats it. The larger the target, the more the coating covers, and the greater the weakness.</p>
  <p>The weakness is equal to the bomb's splash damage, before any status or other bonuses are applied, plus 2 for each size category the target is larger than Medium. A Large creature is one size category larger than Medium, a Huge creature two and a Gargantuan creature three. A creature of Medium size or smaller has no size increase, but still gains the weakness equal to the bomb's splash damage. For example, a Volatile Vial that deals 3 splash damage gives its target weakness 3 to splash damage if it is Medium, 5 if it is Large, 7 if it is Huge and 9 if it is Gargantuan.</p>
  <p>The weakness is applied only on a successful Strike or a critical hit, not on a miss. It affects only the target of your Strike and does not apply to any secondary creature in the splash area. As with any weakness, it is not increased on a critical hit. If the target already has a weakness to splash damage, use whichever is higher instead of adding them together.</p>
  <div class="stat-block special-block">
    <p class="special-label">Special</p>
    <p>Only one additive can be applied to each bomb you create with Quick Alchemy, but there is no limit on how often you can use Sticky Resins: you can add it to every bomb you create.</p>
  </div>
</div>
