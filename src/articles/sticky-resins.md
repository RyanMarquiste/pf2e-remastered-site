<div class="stat-block" id="sticky-resins">
  <h4 class="stat-block-title">Sticky Resins <span class="stat-block-action">Feat 2</span></h4>
  <div class="stat-block-traits">
    <span class="trait">Alchemist</span>
    <span class="trait">Additive</span>
  </div>
  <p><strong>Trigger</strong> You use Quick Alchemy to create a bomb with the splash trait.</p>
  <p>Using a sticky resin additive, you cause your alchemical bomb's splash to coat and stick to your target on a successful attack. The larger your target, the more the coating covers, and the greater the weakness applied.</p>
  <div class="stat-block results-block">
    <p><strong>Critical Success</strong> The resin coats the target as it does on a success. The weakness is not increased on a critical hit.</p>
    <p><strong>Success</strong> The target gains weakness to splash damage for this attack equal to double the number of damage dice the bomb deals, before any other effect changes its dice. Increase this weakness by the bomb's number of damage dice for each size category the target is larger than Medium.</p>
    <p><strong>Failure</strong> The resin doesn't stick. The target gains no weakness.</p>
  </div>
  <p><em>Example: a bomb with 3 damage dice gives its target weakness 6 if it is Medium or smaller, 9 if it is Large, 12 if it is Huge and 15 if it is Gargantuan.</em></p>
  <p>The sticky resin that makes the bomb cling to its target also dampens the bomb's splash effect, so the bomb's splash area is reduced by 5 feet. A bomb with the normal 5-foot splash area affects only its target.</p>
  <p>The weakness affects only the target of your Strike and does not apply to any secondary creature in the splash area. If the target already has a weakness to splash damage, use whichever is higher instead of adding them together.</p>
  <div class="stat-block special-block">
    <p class="special-label">Special</p>
    <p>Only one additive can be applied to each bomb you create with Quick Alchemy, but there is no limit on how often you can use Sticky Resins: you can add it to every bomb you create.</p>
  </div>
</div>
