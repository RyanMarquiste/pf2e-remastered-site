  <p><strong>Cast</strong> {% actionIcon "two" %}</p>
  <p><strong>Range</strong> 30 feet</p>
  <p>A zombie bursts from the terrain beneath or next to a creature of your choice within range. It is fleeting (see the Fleeting trait). Unlike other summoned creatures, it immediately acts using only 1 action, as its shambling, undead form is too slow to muster the usual two &mdash; it makes a melee Strike against that creature using your spell attack modifier, dealing 1d8 bludgeoning damage. On a critical hit, the zombie drags the target partway into the terrain, and the target is immobilized until the start of your next turn. It can use Escape against your spell DC to free itself sooner.</p>
  <p><strong>Heightened (+2)</strong> The damage increases by 1d8.</p>
