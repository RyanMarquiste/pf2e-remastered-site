  <p><strong>Cast</strong> {% actionIcon "two" %}</p>
  <p><strong>Range</strong> 30 feet; <strong>Speed</strong> 30 feet (fly)</p>
  <p>You conjure a writhing shred of unlife in an unoccupied space within range. It is fleeting (see the Fleeting trait) and immediately acts using its own 2 actions, typically flying toward a creature and making a melee Strike against it using your spell attack modifier, dealing 1d6 void damage. On a hit, the target becomes enfeebled 1 until the end of its next turn. On a critical hit, it becomes enfeebled 2 instead, as the shade saps its strength.</p>
  <p><strong>Heightened (+1)</strong> The damage increases by 1d4.</p>
