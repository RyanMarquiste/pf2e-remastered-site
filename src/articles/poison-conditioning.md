<div class="stat-block" id="poison-conditioning">
  <h4 class="stat-block-title">Poison Conditioning <span class="stat-block-action">Feat 4</span></h4>
  <div class="stat-block-traits">
    <span class="trait">Alchemist</span>
    <span class="trait">Additive</span>
  </div>
  <p><strong>Trigger</strong> You use Quick Alchemy to create an item duplicating a named poison.</p>
  <p>You compound the poison into a ward against itself. The resulting item gains the antitoxin trait, loses the poison trait, and no longer functions as a poison. When a creature drinks it, that creature becomes immune to the specific poison it was made from until the end of its next long rest.</p>
  <p><strong>Special</strong> A creature can be under the effect of only one antitoxin at a time; if it would come under the effect of a second, the first ends. This additive can only be applied to a named poison with its own stat block &mdash; it has no effect on generic poison damage or persistent poison damage.</p>
</div>
