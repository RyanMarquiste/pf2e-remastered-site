<div class="stat-block" id="catalytic-crystals">
  <h4 class="stat-block-title">Catalytic Crystals <span class="stat-block-action">Feat 4</span></h4>
  <div class="stat-block-traits">
    <span class="trait">Alchemist</span>
    <span class="trait">Additive</span>
  </div>
  <p><strong>Trigger</strong> You use Quick Alchemy to create a bomb.</p>
  <p>You drop a sprinkling of fine crystals into the bomb that accelerate the chemicals within it. This highly destabilizes the mixture, increasing the bomb's damage but also potentially causing it to explode early.</p>
  <p>Increase the number of damage dice the bomb deals by one.</p>
  <p>If the attack roll with the bomb is a critical failure, or the bomb is not used by the end of the current turn, the bomb explodes in your hand instead of being thrown or becoming inert. You take the bomb's splash damage.</p>
  <div class="stat-block special-block">
    <p class="special-label">Special</p>
    <p>Only one additive can be applied to each bomb you create with Quick Alchemy, but there is no limit on how often you can use Catalytic Crystals: you can add it to every bomb you create.</p>
  </div>
</div>
