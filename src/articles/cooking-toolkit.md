<div class="stat-block" id="cooking-toolkit">
  <h4 class="stat-block-title">Cooking Toolkit <span class="stat-block-action">Item 0</span></h4>
  <p><strong>Price</strong> 3 gp; <strong>Usage</strong> held in 2 hands; <strong>Bulk</strong> 1</p>
  <p>This mobile collection of spices, food packs, and basic cooking implements can be used for quick work in the kitchen or at a campfire. If you wear your cooking toolkit, you can draw and replace it as part of the action that uses it.</p>
</div>

<p>The <a href="{{ 'quick-cooking' | articleUrl(collections.articles) | url }}">Quick Cooking</a> action needs a cooking toolkit, and a Wandering Chef can use one in place of an alchemist's toolkit to craft alchemical foods.</p>
